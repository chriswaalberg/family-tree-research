# Changelog

One line per version from 0.9.38 on; older versions are in the git history (`git log --oneline`).

- **0.9.70** — Wish 43, and the Workshop shows what really changed. A plugin hook: in a tree's folder (`family-tree.json`/`stamboom.json`) every session starts with "load the skill, open the Workshop before the first step, preparation included, and tell it at once what you are doing" (also when the project's own CLAUDE.md explains the research), and every request gets one line saying whether the Workshop is open; nothing outside a tree. `archives.py`, `delpher.py` and `gedcom_tool.py` say once an hour when they run in a tree's folder with its Workshop closed. Onboarding writes those rules into a new tree's CLAUDE.md. New tab **Changes**: only real changes to the tree this session (people, links, certainty, sources and reasons, kind of link, names, dates, stories, photos, occupations, round events and notes), grouped per update with the time, kept by the server (a reopened page or a phone sees all of it, the replay rebuilds it); scrolling lights up the people concerned in the sky, a full-screen button shows the list alone. Workers keep their place (Claude first, helpers in order of arrival). Fix: a `status` step no longer wipes the kind of link (raised/adopted/foster); a round that only set "certain" had removed an adoption.
- **0.9.69** — The Workshop's screen redone for several workers and people outside the fan: a compact header with a menu (☰) for every control and the counts on one line, and under the sky three tabs: Workers (one row per agent: colour, who it is on, its last step; greys out when quiet), Log (readable, coloured per worker, "2 records · Het Utrechts Archief" instead of archive ids) and Outside the fan (with a count; rejections crossed out). Beams are predictable: one per worker with its name, pointing at its person and staying there, a full circle only for a check of the whole tree, gone when the worker is quiet. A reopened page knows again who is on whom.
- **0.9.68** — Fix: with the Wi-Fi on by default, a second tree's Workshop could slip onto the same port as the first (on 127.0.0.1 next to 0.0.0.0) and the laptop then talked to the wrong tree. A taken port now always means the next port; `launch` checks ports for the Wi-Fi too.
- **0.9.67** — The phone can always watch: the Workshop listens on the Wi-Fi by default (with the tree's key), so the phone button shows its QR code without asking Claude for a restart; the address is worked out on every tap (another network, still right). `--local` keeps it to this computer; the old `--lan` is ignored, so existing launch entries still start.
- **0.9.66** — Workshop: an EN/NL switch like the viewer's, sharing the reader's choice with it (switch in one, the other opens in that language too); without a choice it follows the tree's language. Header buttons stay on one line on a 375 px phone.
- **0.9.65** — Workshop: a replay button plays the whole session again from the tree as it was at the start (every search, note, check and round, same animations), with pause, 1×/2×/4× and back to live; the server keeps every event and data.json version of the session in memory. The QR code is in the Workshop's own style: a dark card with light round dots and clay eyes. A star that already shows its name gets only the "working on" ring.
- **0.9.64** — The Workshop on a phone without typing: a phone button at the top shows a QR code of the phone address (only on the computer that runs it). The key in that address is now fixed per tree (kept in `~/.config/family-tree-research/`, never in the tree's folder) and the phone remembers it for 30 days, so a bookmark keeps working after a restart. Without `--lan` the button says what to ask Claude.
- **0.9.63** — Workshop: the source card shows one short line per record (the description is the link, no long addresses; "+ n more" after three), addresses in the log become the site name; the empty "Working on" bar no longer shows when nobody is being worked on.
- **0.9.62** — You see who is being worked on: a ring and the name at that person's star (one per helper, in its colour) and "Working on" above the log, from `live.py focus` (new) and from every search with a name. Names are recognised with spelling variants (Marritje = Maritje = Marretje), two given names or a surname only one ancestor has; someone without a star shows at their nearest relative ("wife of Jan de Vries"). The radar never freezes: animations run on a clock with a timer fallback, so a hidden window or a busy computer can't stop them or the queue, and the sweep no longer measures the page for every slot. On narrow screens and in the side panel the page fills the window: the sky up to square, the log down to the bottom edge.
- **0.9.61** — Wish 32: one Workshop per tree. A second tree gets its own port, opening a running one reuses it (exit 0), pointers of stopped Workshops are cleaned up, and events only go to the Workshop of the tree you work in (the current folder, or the file rebuild/check writes; `--tree`/`FTR_TREE` from elsewhere). New `live.py launch` writes the `workshop` entry in the tree's `.claude/launch.json` with the current plugin path and the tree's port; the skill then shows the side panel and checks it is the right tree (`/ping`, tab title). Unchecked constellation lines are visible in the Cosmos.
- **0.9.60** — The Workshop opens in the Cosmos: ancestors as stars in fixed places (father's side left, one orbit per generation), constellation lines in the colour of their certainty, ghost stars for missing parents, names up to the great-grandparents; tap a star to see who it is (also in the Fan). The Fan stays one tap away; each browser remembers the choice.
- **0.9.59** — Workshop, tested on a real tree of 1,242 people: much lighter (no glow filter or endless animation per slot, the particle layer only runs while something moves; it slowed whole computers down in 0.9.58). Colours closer to the Claude desktop app (charcoal and clay). New places for research that isn't adding people: a source or reason added to links (one line, they light up together), every new note and event of the last round from `rebuild` (also "searched, nothing found"), updated stories, life and times per place, `note --kind nothing`, and a bar with certain / probable / possible / not checked. Many certainty changes at once play together.
- **0.9.58** — The Workshop (Claude Code only): `scripts/live.py serve` opens a local page (`viewer/live.html`) with the start person's fan as a night sky, in the desktop app's side panel or on a phone (`--lan`, address with a secret key). Every new data.json is compared with the previous one and played: new ancestors fly in from their source, rejected links crack, links that became certain turn gold, open questions breathe. `archives.py`, `delpher.py` and `gedcom_tool.py check/rebuild` report what they do by themselves; `live.py note` and `live.py progress` (progress bar with time left) for everything else; helpers with `FTR_WHO=<name>` get their own scanner beam. Built for narrow screens. `references/workshop.md`.
- **0.9.55** — The Fan is round: the start person in the middle, father's side left, mother's side right, every ring 0.8 × the one inside it, text in the lower half turned upright. On a phone the segments are two to three times as wide as in the half fan, on a desktop about 1.3 to 1.5 times. The README screenshot shows the round fan.
- **0.9.48–0.9.54** — Research tools from another session: `archives.py` (open archives without a browser, OA scans), rebuilding the tree from the original plus round files with fixed ids, stricter evidence rules; see `git log`.
- **0.9.47** — The generations slider goes as far as the start person's tree is deep (no fixed 12/14 any more), for Fan, Cosmos and Timeline. Fix: with the line off, an empty bar stood above every tree view since 0.9.43.
- **0.9.46** — The Story line is shown whole in every tree view, also when it is longer than the generations slider allows (Timeline 14, Fan/Cosmos 12): past the slider only the line goes on. In Fan a thin line with dots follows the line where its slices get thinner than a pixel; Timeline zooms and scrolls so the whole line fits.
- **0.9.45** — A person's portrait stands as a polaroid at the top right of their block in Story (square corners, wide white bottom with the photo's title, a slight tilt; above the name on phones); other photos and documents stay small. Which photo: `_PRIM Y` in the GEDCOM (from Ancestry/FTM/RootsMagic, or the new `portrait` column of media.csv via `gedcom_tool.py media`), else the first photo. Cards uses the same portrait.
- **0.9.44** — "Last updated" shows the time too ("22 November 2026, 2:35 pm" / "22 november 2026, 14:35"; `bijgewerkt` is now date + time, a plain date still works). Nicknames and other names (`NICK`, a second `NAME` such as `TYPE aka`) go into `aka`: search finds them and the viewer shows "Pieter Johannes de Vries (Piet)". A story place's `bijschrift` now shows under its map in Story and Cards instead of the template sentence; a story place without `jaar` says "Part of the story takes place in …" instead of "lived there for a while". `delpher.py`: the `--check` image veils everything outside the box (so a misplaced box shows at once) and the grid numbers are printed on the scan too (reading image pixels instead of the ruler was ~70 px off).
- **0.9.43** — "In the tree" in Story's route bar shows the trail you are reading in every tree view (Family, Line, Timeline, Fan, Cosmos): the rest of the tree fades, Family opens and zooms to fit the line, Fan/Cosmos/Timeline get enough generations. A bar above the tree goes back to the story or hides the line; the line follows Story and survives the Back button.
- **0.9.41** — The viewer remembers the start person the reader picked (name in the title, search, "Make start person") and opens there next time, per browser and per tree; a link to a person still wins.
- **0.9.40** — The viewer's footer says when the tree was last updated ("Last updated: 22 November 2026" / "Laatst bijgewerkt: …"): `make_viewer_data.py` writes `bijgewerkt` (today, or `--updated YYYY-MM-DD`, `--updated none` to leave it out).
- **0.9.38** — Newspaper clippings come into the Story view by themselves: the skill asks for a clipping (`soort: "krant"`) for every newspaper item a story quotes or leans on, and puts other finds in `media.csv` as documents; `make_viewer_data.py` warns "this story cites a newspaper without a clipping"; new `scripts/delpher.py` (search Delpher via SRU, read the OCR, crop with a ruler and cut a clipping); `references/sources.md` explains the Delpher SRU route.
